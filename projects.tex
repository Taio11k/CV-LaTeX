\projEntry{\href{https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dSFxPbqb8e6QTG37ltxU5vcMvSJfFY8U/view?usp=sharing}{Image Processing for a Bionic Eye: Retinal Encoder Evaluation}}
{Jul 2025 -- Jun 2026}
{Final Year Project (Honours), graded 88, High Distinction}
{
\begin{itemize}
    \item Built a retinal encoder and phosphene-rendering pipeline in Python with Pulse2Percept. The model covers photoreceptor filtering, centre-surround receptive fields, contrast gain control and ON/OFF pathways, and turns camera frames into simulated bionic-eye percepts.
    \item Designed and ran a 15-participant perceptual study under Monash ethics approval MUHREC 44457: four encoder configurations, three custom recognition tasks, 60 randomised trials.
    \item Found spatial resolution to be the dominant driver of recognition accuracy. The best configuration reached 69.2\% mean accuracy against 55.1\% for the coarsest, a 14-point gap between a 625- and a 144-electrode array.
\end{itemize}
}
